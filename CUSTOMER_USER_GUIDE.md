## Getting Started

### What is The Sovereign Blade?

The Sovereign Blade is a **modern barbershop queue management system** that allows you to:
- ✅ Book your appointment online
- ✅ Self-register as a walk-in customer
- ✅ Track your position in real-time
- ✅ Know your estimated wait time
- ✅ Receive SMS notifications
- ✅ Make advance payments

### System Requirements

**To use our website:**
- 📱 Smartphone or computer
- 🌐 Internet connection (3G/4G or WiFi)
- 📧 Valid phone number (10-digit Indian mobile)

**Supported Browsers:**
- Chrome (recommended)
- Safari
- Firefox
- Edge

**Mobile Devices:**
- iPhone 7+
- Android 6.0+

## Joining the Queue

### Step 1: Visit Our Website

```
1. Open your browser

3. Click "Book Online" button
```

### Step 2: Fill in Your Details

```
Screen 1 of 3 — Select Your Services
├─ Choose services you want (up to 3)
├─ See pricing and duration
├─ Total shows at right
└─ Click "Continue"
```

**Available Services:**
- 💇 **Classic Haircut** — 30 min — ₹500

```

### Step 4: Enter Your Information

```

## Tracking Your Status

### View Your Queue Position

After joining, you can track your status anytime:

**Method 1: From Email/SMS**
```
1. Click link in SMS: "Track your status"
2. Or manually visit: https://sovereignblade.vercel.app/status/YOUR-TOKEN
```

**Method 2: Save the Link**
```
1. After joining: Save your unique status link
2. Bookmark it in browser
3. Visit anytime to see updated status
```

### Understanding Your Status

#### Status 1️⃣: **WAITING**
```
✅ You've joined the queue
📍 Position: #3
⏱️ Your turn in: ~25 minutes
📱 Waiting for your turn
```

**What to do:**
- Keep your phone nearby
- You'll receive SMS when approaching
- Monitor the waiting room display screen

#### Status 2️⃣: **APPROACHING** (Get Ready!)
```
⚠️ Position: #2
✨ Your turn is next!
🔔 You'll be called within 5 minutes
```

**What to do:**
- Get ready
- Watch for your token on waiting room screen
- Listen for announcement
- Get to the barber chair

#### Status 3️⃣: **CALLED** (Your Turn!)
```
🎉 IT'S YOUR TURN!
📢 Your token called
👨 Proceed to: Rajan Mehta
⏰ Barber ready for you
```

**What to do:**
- 🎯 Go to your barber's chair
- Provide your token (if asked)
- Ready for your service

#### Status 4️⃣: **IN SERVICE**
```
💇 Currently being served
⏱️ Service started at: 2:15 PM
⏰ Estimated duration: 30 minutes
```

**What to do:**
- Relax and enjoy the service
- Your service is in progress
- Estimated finish time shown

#### Status 5️⃣: **COMPLETED**
```
✅ Service complete!
👏 Thank you for visiting
⭐ Share your feedback
📱 Book your next appointment
```

**What to do:**
- 🎉 Enjoy your haircut!
- Settle payment
- Leave feedback (optional)
- Book next appointment

#### Status 6️⃣: **CANCELLED**
```
❌ Your appointment cancelled
📱 Contact us for more info
📞 Call: [salon-number]
```

**What to do:**
- If cancelled by mistake, contact salon
- Book a new appointment

#### Status 7️⃣: **NO-SHOW**
```
⚠️ You didn't appear for appointment
📱 Call us to reschedule
👉 Avoid future no-shows
```

**What to do:**
- If legitimate reason: contact salon
- Apologize and reschedule
- Future tip: Set phone reminders

## Canceling Your Appointment

### How to Cancel

```
Your Status Page → "Cancel My Spot" Button
1. Click "Cancel My Spot"
2. Confirm cancellation
3. You're removed from queue
4. No refund needed (you didn't pay yet)
```

**If you paid in advance:**
- Automatic refund within 24 hours
- Refund sent to original payment method
- Contact us for instant refund request

### Cancellation Policy

✅ **Can cancel anytime:**
- While waiting
- Full refund (if paid)
- No cancellation fees

❌ **Cannot cancel:**
- When "In Service" (already started)
- After "Completed" (service done)

### What Happens After Cancellation

```
1. Your token becomes inactive
2. Your position removed from queue
3. Everyone behind you moves up
4. New position calculated for all
5. If paid: Refund process starts
```

## FAQ

### General Questions

#### Q1: Do I need an account to book?

**A:** No! The Sovereign Blade is completely anonymous:
- ✅ No login required
- ✅ No passwords
- ✅ No registration
- ✅ Just use your phone number

#### Q2: Can I change my appointment?

**A:** Currently not supported. Instead:
1. Cancel your current appointment
2. Create a new appointment
3. You'll get a new token

#### Q3: What if I don't get my token?

**A:** Keep the status link or token number. You can:
1. Visit: https://sovereignblade.vercel.app
2. Click "Check Status"
3. Enter your token to find position
4. Or ask receptionist

#### Q4: Can multiple people book under one phone number?

**A:** No. The system prevents duplicate active bookings:
- 🚫 One active booking per phone number
- ✅ You must complete current booking to create new one
- 💡 Use different family members' numbers if needed

#### Q5: How accurate is the wait time estimate?

**A:** Pretty accurate! We calculate based on:
- 📊 Average service duration
- 👥 Number of barbers available
- ⏱️ Time of day (peak vs. off-peak)
- 🧮 Total people ahead of you

**Reality check:** Estimates ±5-10 minutes (normal variation)

### Technical Questions

#### Q6: I lost my token. How do I find it?

**A:** You have several options:
1. **Check SMS history** — Look for original confirmation SMS
2. **Check status link** — Did you bookmark it?
3. **Ask receptionist** — Provide your phone number
4. **Rejoin** — Start a new booking with same phone

#### Q7: My location shows offline. Can I still use the app?

**A:** Yes! The system works with:
- ✅ 3G/4G mobile data
- ✅ WiFi
- ✅ Works even if location permission disabled
- 📍 Location NOT required

#### Q8: Can I use the system outside India?

**A:** Our current setup:
- ✅ Website: Accessible worldwide
- ❌ SMS notifications: India only (Indian phone numbers)
- ❌ Payments: India only (Razorpay)

#### Q9: Is my phone number secure?

**A:** Yes! Your data is protected:
- 🔒 Only used for SMS notifications
- 🔒 Never sold or shared
- 🔒 Encrypted in database
- 🔒 Deleted after 30 days (if no activity)

#### Q10: What if the WiFi disconnects during booking?

**A:** Don't worry:
- Your booking is saved on our server
- Try again when connection restored
- If you got a token, you're booked
- Check SMS or status page

## Tips & Tricks

### Pro Tips for Best Experience

✅ **Tip 1: Book During Off-Peak Hours**
- Best times: 11 AM - 2 PM, 5 PM - 6 PM (weekdays)
- Avoid: 1 PM - 3 PM, 6 PM - 8 PM (peak)
- Wait time: 5-10 minutes (off-peak) vs 30+ (peak)

✅ **Tip 2: Use UPI Payment**
- Save 2% vs credit card
- Instant confirmation
- Directly from your bank account
- Recommended by us!

✅ **Tip 3: Save Your Status Link**
- Bookmark the link
- Check status anytime
- Get accurate wait time
- Share with friends

✅ **Tip 4: Set Phone Reminders**
- When you join: Set reminder for ETA
- When approaching: Get ready
- Reduces no-shows and lateness

✅ **Tip 5: Request Specific Barber**
- Some barbers specialize in certain styles
- Book with preferred barber
- Leads to better results
- Ask receptionist for recommendations

### Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Mistake 1: Wrong Phone Number**
- Double-check before booking
- You won't receive SMS notifications
- Can't retrieve booking without correct number

❌ **Mistake 2: Forgetting Your Token**
- Save SMS or bookmark status link
- Or call salon to find your token
- Always keep phone on you

❌ **Mistake 3: Missing Your Turn**
- Keep phone on and nearby
- Watch waiting room display
- Listen for announcements
- Don't go too far while waiting

❌ **Mistake 4: Booking During Break**
- Check if barber is available
- Some take breaks 1-2 PM
- Choose different barber or time

❌ **Mistake 5: Arriving Too Early**
- Arrive close to your time
- Waiting room might be crowded
- Barber readiness calculated
- Arrive within 10 min of estimated time
